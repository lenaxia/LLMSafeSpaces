## Key Decisions

1. **Strip, don't delete**: the scrub removes KEY MATERIAL, preserving file structure and sibling fields — user-visible damage surface is minimal and idempotency is structural.
2. **The trigger is the batch's relay presence** (the same signal the US-72.4 monitor reads): flag-off pods never scrub (their raw keys are the working path); the sync.Once bounds it to once per pod lifetime.
