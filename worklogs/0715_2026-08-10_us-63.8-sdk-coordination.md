# Worklog: US-63.8 — SDK Coordination

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Epic:** 63 (Inboard Session Queue)
**Status:** Complete

---

## Objective

Align all four SDKs (Go, TypeScript, Python, Java) with the V2 session-
queue model. Mark `listQueue` and `dismissQueued` as deprecated; document
the event-derived alternative.

---

## Work Completed

- **Go SDK**: `ListQueue` and `DismissQueued` marked `Deprecated:` in doc
  comments. `QueuedMessage` doc notes `RetryCount` is vestigial under V2.
- **TypeScript SDK**: `listQueue` and `dismissQueued` marked `@deprecated`
  in JSDoc.
- **Python SDK**: `list_queue` and `dismiss_queued` marked with
  `.. deprecated::` docstring directives.
- **Java SDK**: `dismissQueued` marked `@Deprecated` annotation + Javadoc.
  (No `listQueue` method exists in the Java SDK.)
- **`sdks/README.md`**: new "Session Queue (Epic 63)" section documenting
  both modes, the deprecation timeline, and the SSE event-derived
  alternative for SDK consumers needing reliable queue visibility.
