## Finding

The story spec described removing Epic 41's client-side drain-on-idle
machinery (`notifyIdle`/`drainOne`/`_retryCount`). Inspection of the
current frontend reveals this machinery was already removed in a prior
refactor. `useMessageQueue.ts` is purely event-driven:

- Pills derived from `queue.update` SSE events via `refreshQueue()`
- Fresh-load handled by `refreshQueue()` on mount (`GET /queue`)
- No `notifyIdle`, `drainOne`, or `_retryCount` in `frontend/src/`
- Mode-agnostic: works identically under V1 (msgqueue) and V2 (SSE
  bridge + shadow marker)

All acceptance criteria met by existing architecture. Story marked
Complete with verification note in the design doc.
